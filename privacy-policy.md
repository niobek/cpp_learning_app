# Privacy Policy

**Learn Code: Cpp Swift Sql** respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or messages.

Lesson progress, earned XP, hearts, medals, and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

## Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the app is used and improve its features and performance. Firebase may process limited technical and usage information, such as:

* App interactions and events
* Device and operating system information
* App version and general performance information
* Anonymous identifiers

This information is not used by us to identify you personally.

You can learn more about how Google handles information at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Purchases and Subscriptions

The app uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchase status. RevenueCat may process information related to:

* An anonymous app user identifier
* Subscription and purchase status
* Product identifiers
* Transaction and receipt information

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

You can learn more about RevenueCat’s privacy practices at:

https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

You can learn more about Apple’s privacy practices at:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Limited technical, analytics, and purchase-related information may be processed by Firebase, RevenueCat, and Apple only to provide, maintain, and improve the app and its subscription features.

## Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The app does not provide user accounts, public profiles, messaging, or user-generated content.

## Data Retention

Learning progress stored locally remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to their respective privacy policies.

## Your Choices

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device. Subscription management and cancellation are available through your Apple Account subscription settings.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub page.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
