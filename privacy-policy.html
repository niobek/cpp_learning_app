Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Learn Code: Cpp Swift Sql respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or messages. Third-party services integrated into the app may collect technical, usage, advertising, and purchase-related information as described below.

Lesson progress, earned XP, medals, lessons unlocked by watching rewarded ads, and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the app is used and improve its features. Firebase may process technical and usage information, including:

  • App interactions and events
  • Device and operating system information
  • App version
  • App-instance identifiers

We use this information to understand usage patterns rather than to identify you by name.

Learn more about Google’s Privacy Policy .

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads. Completing a rewarded ad unlocks the selected lesson. Pro users can access lessons without watching ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process information including:

  • IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them
  • Device, app, or advertising identifiers, where available
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Ad views, video views, and other advertising interactions

This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services. Ad personalization depends on applicable requirements, your privacy choices, and device permissions.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices and present privacy messages where required.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy .

Purchases and Subscriptions

The app uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchase status. RevenueCat may process information related to:

  • An app user identifier generated without requiring an account
  • Subscription and purchase status
  • Product identifiers
  • Transaction and receipt information

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Learn more about RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy .

Data Sharing

Information may be processed by Google Firebase, Google AdMob and its advertising partners, RevenueCat, and Apple for the analytics, advertising, purchase management, security, and operational purposes described in this policy.

Advertising services may share device identifiers and advertising information with advertising partners, depending on your choices, device permissions, and the services used. Their respective privacy policies explain their data practices.

Children’s Privacy

The app does not provide user accounts, public profiles, messaging, or user-generated content. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

As described above, integrated analytics and advertising services may automatically process technical and usage information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email address below.

Data Retention

Local learning progress and preferences generally remain on your device until you delete the app or its data. Device backups may retain or restore locally stored information.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to their respective privacy policies. Deleting the app does not automatically delete information held by those services.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to watch rewarded ads. Pro access allows you to access lessons without watching ads.

Where an advertising privacy message is presented, you can make the choices offered in that message. Device privacy permissions can also be managed through iOS Settings.

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device. Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription. Subscription management and cancellation are available through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion, or objection to certain processing. Contact us with requests concerning information we hold. For information held independently by third-party providers, their privacy policies describe available request options.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
