Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or messages.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the app is used and improve its features and performance. Firebase may process limited technical and usage information, including:

  • App interactions and events

Purchases and Subscriptions

The app uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchase status. RevenueCat may process information related to:

  • An anonymous app user identifier

  • Product identifiers
  • Transaction and receipt information

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Learn more about RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy .

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Limited technical, analytics, and purchase-related information may be processed by Firebase, RevenueCat, and Apple only to provide, maintain, and improve the app and its subscription features.

Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The app does not provide user accounts, public profiles, messaging, or user-generated content.

Data Retention

Learning progress stored locally remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to their respective privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device. Subscription management and cancellation are available through your Apple Account subscription settings.
